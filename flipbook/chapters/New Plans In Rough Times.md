# New Plans In Rough Times

The alarms aboard the [Starship GemOS] did not sing; they shrieked.

For days, the relentless crew of this brand-new, faster-than-light vessel had been nursing a dying giant. Their state-of-the-art lightspeed engines were overheating, radiating a lethal thermal bloom throughout the engineering decks. Worse, the primary filtration grids had ruptured, plunging the crew into a desperate state of water scarcity.

Spurred by survival, they dropped out of FTL into the gravity well of [Gemikron]—a colossal, Jupiter-sized planet flanked by two jagged, resource-rich moons. It was supposed to be a quick triage: harvest glacial runoff from the moons, vent the engine cores, and slip back into the dark.

Instead, they crossed the threshold straight into a war zone.

[Gemikron] was crawling with a hostile, rogue paramilitary faction. Operating as a fierce resistance group across the twin moons, these combatants had turned the system’s skies into a graveyard of burning metal. The crew of the [Starship GemOS] now faced a terrifying paradox. To cool their engines and quench their thirst, they had to descend directly into the crossfire of a brutal planetary conflict.

Commander Vance stared at the tactical telemetry as the ship’s hulls groaned under atmospheric pressure. “We don’t have the water to freeze the manifolds, and we don’t have the propulsion to run,” she muttered.

Every drop of moisture was vanishing. The engines were minutes from a catastrophic meltdown. To survive, the relentless crew would have to outmaneuver a desperate military force that saw the high-tech [Starship GemOS] not as a vessel in distress, but as the ultimate prize.

The hunt for water on [Gemikron] had officially begun.
